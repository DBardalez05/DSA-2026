# Assignment 1

## Learning Strategies

### 1. A successful learning experience

One time learning went really well for me was in PIE, where I got to create my own PCB and debug issues on real hardware. Facing problems along the way helped me understand what was happening and why. That hands-on experience made the material stick because I was applying it to something I was building.


### 2. Approaches that have not worked for me

I find it harder to learn in classes where a practice set is assigned and due by the next class. The short turnaround can make me focus more on finishing for a grade than fully understanding the material. When I get stuck, I sometimes feel like I have to sacrifice the time I need to understand something just to meet the deadline.


### 3. My perspective on AI and learning

I agree with this framing. I will likely use AI in this course, but I want to use it in ways that support my learning. For example, I could ask it to help format a README before submitting or clarify a topic I am struggling with. When debugging, I would ask, “Explain the error I got,” rather than “Fix my code,” so I still have to understand the problem and work through the solution myself.

### 4. My plan for this course

Time management will be especially important because I am learning C++ alongside the course. After this first assignment, I realize I cannot leave the work until the last couple of days. I plan to start assignments earlier so I have enough time to learn unfamiliar concepts and work through problems. I also want to make small projects using new things I learn and continue practicing on LeetCode to reinforce those skills.

### 5. My thoughts on the oral quizzes

I think the proposed oral quiz activities are useful because they could show whether I can apply what I have learned on the spot. The one I would consider removing is explaining my own code, since that feels easier to prepare for than the other activities. I would find tasks involving unfamiliar code or a new problem more helpful for checking whether I can apply my understanding beyond something I have already practiced.


### 6. Support I would find helpful

The teaching team could best support me by being available when I have questions or get stuck.
